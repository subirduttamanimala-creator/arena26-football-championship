06 / MATCH-DAY QUESTIONS

Know the
rules.

Answers for the prototype. Confirm the official tournament rules with your faculty coordinator.

Who can enter? +

The prototype shows ten section slots for each of four academic years. Actual eligibility and player requirements must be set by the college.

Are these real team registrations? +

No. Team names and registration statuses are fictional demo data created to show how the tournament page could look.

Does submitting the form notify the organizer? +

No. This single-file prototype stores entries in the current browser only. It has no shared database, email notifications or server.

How are teams grouped? +

The fixture plan is illustrative. The organizers should decide group sizes, seeding, tie-breakers, match duration and knockout qualification before publishing official fixtures.

Can I export registrations? +

Yes. The demo can export entries saved in this browser to a CSV file. It does not include sample teams as submitted registrations.
